from Google Material Symbols:

- `keyboard_double_arrow_up` (Jump)
- `sprint` (Dash)
- `touch_app` (neutral touch prompt)
- `book` (Journal/Special)

`grab-ungrabbed.svg` and `grab-grabbed.svg` use the icon geometry from
the free-attribution Noun Project download for the `punch` artwork by
[Daniel Tacho](https://thenounproject.com/creator/danieltacho/), including
icon [5229550](https://thenounproject.com/icon/punch-5229550/). The icon is
used under the [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
The downloaded SVG's attribution footer was moved into this notice so it does
not become part of the in-game control glyph. Changes: the artwork is cropped
to its original 100 x 100 icon canvas, assigned semantic Grab-state filenames,
and rasterized as a monochrome alpha mask. Copyright Daniel Tacho.

`pause.svg` and the remaining touch-control artwork are simple project-owned
geometry rendered by the app. Noun Project and Google do not endorse this
project.
